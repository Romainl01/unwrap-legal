## Common questions

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Unwrap, and tap Cancel Subscription. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

**I paid but the app still shows the paywall.**
Open Unwrap, go to Settings, and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you paid with.

**How do I turn the spicy cards off?**
In Settings, open Your mix and untick Hot. Hot cards are for adults only and are never on unless you choose them.

**How do I erase my data?**
In Settings, tap Erase everything. Your names, answers and scratched surprises live on your phone, so this removes them for good. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy) for what the app sends and why.
